Hello! I'm Avery Reyna.

I'm a Software Engineer at Capital One, building metric visualization tools for a platform that monitors data quality across the enterprise. Before that, I received my BS at the University of Central Florida with minors in Cultural Anthropology, Political Science, and Sociology. I was able to collaborate with Amy Donley, Pamela Wisniewski, and Jonathan Powell researching applied sociology, human-computer interaction, and computational social science. While doing research, I worked at places like the Progressive Turnout Project, the Council on Foreign Relations, New America, and ActBlue. Outside of work, I am studying agentic coding workflows with the Foundation Interface Lab at UC San Diego and rejoining the CoupCast project now under the University of Kentucky. In my free time, I'm documenting my thoughts with Field Notes, writing about music under Magdalena Baywatch, and building experimental prototypes as Self Adjoint Operator.
