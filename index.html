About CV Projects Resources

I'm a Software Engineer at Capital One working in data quality management and an Independent Researcher at the Foundation Interface Lab at UC San Diego currently studying AI-assisted programming via generative UIs. Previously, I investigated the agentic coding workflows of experienced developers under the direction of Brian Hempel. Before that, I received my Social Sciences degree at the University of Central Florida with minors in Cultural Anthropology, Political Science, and Sociology. I was able to work with Amy Donley, Pamela Wisniewski, and Jonathan Powell researching applied sociology, human-computer interaction, and computational social science, respectively. In between classes, I interned at places like the Council on Foreign Relations, The Hub Project, Swing Left, New America, and ActBlue. I spent my summers completing REUs at the University of Washington Tacoma with Jim Thatcher and Columbia University in Brian Smith's CEAL Lab. In my free time, I'm writing under Semantic Distance and prototyping as Self Adjoint Operator.

Research Interests

I'm interested in making software construction more intuitive by researching the intersections between artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and software engineering. Looking below, here are a couple of research directions I want to explore:

Malleable Interfaces
Is it possible to rebuild the current interface paradigm of creative software to enable users to make more?
Programming Languages for Scientists
How can we lower the barrier to entry for scientists to incorporate programming into their workflows?
Developer Experience with Agents
How can AI better assist programmers in their development process without sacrificing their agency?
