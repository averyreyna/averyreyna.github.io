About CV Projects Resources

I'm a Software Engineer at Capital One, building metric visualization tools for a platform that monitors data quality across the enterprise. Before that, I received my BS at the University of Central Florida with minors in Cultural Anthropology, Political Science, and Sociology. I was able to collaborate with Amy Donley, Pamela Wisniewski, and Jonathan Powell studying applied sociology, human-computer interaction, and computational social science, respectively. In between classes and research, I worked at places like the Council on Foreign Relations, The Hub Project, Swing Left, New America, and ActBlue. Outside of work, I am studying agentic coding workflows with the Foundation Interface Lab at UC San Diego and rejoining the CoupCast project, now under the maintenance of the University of Kentucky. In my free time, I'm documenting my thoughts with Semantic Distance, writing about music under Magdalena Baywatch, and building prototypes as Self Adjoint Operator.
