Hello! I'm Avery Reyna.

I'm a Software Engineer at Capital One, building metric visualization tools for a platform that monitors data quality across the enterprise. Before that, I received my BS at the University of Central Florida with minors in Cultural Anthropology, Political Science, and Sociology. I had the privilege of working with Amy Donley, Pamela Wisniewski, and Jonathan Powell researching urban sociology, human-computer interaction, and computational social science. While doing research, I worked at places like the Progressive Turnout Project, the Council on Foreign Relations, New America, and ActBlue. In my free time, I am conducting field research, starting experiments, and building prototypes as Self Adjoint Operator.
