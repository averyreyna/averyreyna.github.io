True American Grand Slam

With Sinner out of the US Open after sustaining (and recovering from) a right knee injury, this is the best bet for an American to win a Grand Slam since Roddick at Wimbledon 2009. I believe Alcaraz is still slated to play, but even if he attends, the aforementioned chances are still high up there.

Tiafoe looks to be in good... (more like okay...) shape. His Cincinnati QF match against Musetti was all about capitalization since the Italian has insane physicality, causing balls you thought were winners to be put back in play (which happened like five times during the second set alone.) I am a little worried considering his first serve was not there, with Tiafoe only making 44% of them in. Granted, for the first serves he made in, he won 89% of the points, which is a little insane.

If Alcaraz is not on the top of his game, it's a wrap. He CANNOT survive five-setters coming back from injury, even though I do think the discourse surrounding his absence is a little overblown. We (also) forget another Spaniard has suffered pretty heartbreaking injuries, with the beginning of his career characterized by withstanding foot pain—even at Roland Garros.

Edit after Gauff's match wrapped up: she can definitely win the US Open, Sabalenka is already on a rough patch, so the best time to find your best tennis is now. Then again, we can't count her out just yet, I mean look at Swiatek! Imagine telling us after her losing to Eala at Wimbledon this year that she would win Toronto beating Rybakina in the final... yeah I would also have that reaction.
