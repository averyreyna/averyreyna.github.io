More on CRDTs

Back to Matt Weidner's CRDT survey, picking up the thread from much earlier in the year. Part 1 nails down what a CRDT even is (a distributed algorithm that computes an app's state from its operation history) and part 2 reframes that as a pure function over an unordered set of operations, where unique ids and the causal order are the machinery that makes convergence work. From there part 2 builds outward into the semantic techniques themselves: ordered list and text positions, registers that either pick a last writer or keep every conflict, and the object composition that assembles these primitives into whole apps without losing the guarantees.
