Continuity

A function f : X → Y between topological spaces is continuous if the preimage of every open set in Y is open in X, generalizing the ε–δ definition from metric spaces to arbitrary topological spaces.

  • A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with a continuous inverse (the isomorphisms of Top)
  • Continuous maps are the morphisms in the category Top, making Top a proper category
  • Homotopy: two continuous maps f, g : X → Y are homotopic if one can be continuously deformed into the other; homotopy equivalence is coarser than homeomorphism, important in algebraic topology

The categorical framing: continuous maps preserve open sets, functors preserve structure. Both are morphisms in their respective categories. This connection deepens through the Curry–Howard–Lambek correspondence in type theory.
