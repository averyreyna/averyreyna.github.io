Galois Connections

A Galois connection between two partially ordered sets (C, ≤) and (A, ⊑) is a pair of monotone functions α : C → A and γ : A → C satisfying α(c) ⊑ a ⇔ c ≤ γ(a).

  • α is the abstraction function, mapping concrete values to abstract ones
  • γ is the concretization function, mapping abstract values back to sets of concrete values
  • Galois connections are the mathematical core of abstract interpretation: they formalize what it means for an abstraction to be sound
  • The sign abstraction has α mapping integers to {−, 0, +} and γ mapping each sign to the corresponding integers
  • Galois connections are adjunctions in the category of partially ordered sets, directly connecting abstract interpretation to category theory

Every sound static analysis corresponds to a Galois connection. The adjunction perspective shows why: abstraction and concretization are left and right adjoints.
