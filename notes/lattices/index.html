Lattices

A lattice is a partially ordered set where every pair of elements has a least upper bound (join, ∨) and greatest lower bound (meet, ∧).

  • The powerset of any set ordered by ⊆ is a lattice: union is join, intersection is meet
  • A complete lattice has joins and meets for arbitrary subsets, not just pairs
  • The natural numbers under divisibility form a lattice: lcm is join, gcd is meet
  • Lattices appear throughout computer science: type hierarchies, program analysis domains, and the consistency models underlying CRDTs

Every CRDT state space is a join-semilattice. The merge function is the join operation. This is why lattice theory is the mathematical backbone of CRDTs.
