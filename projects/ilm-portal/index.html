ILM Portal

August 16, 2024

A centralized identity and lifecycle management portal that brings together all the plugins our team was building under one roof instead of bouncing between disconnected tools. I led the end-to-end design on this one, serving as the point of contact for all UX concerns and ensuring the experience held together as we onboarded new plugins. One of the bigger ones it housed was a toxic access pairs plugin I helped redesign, which flagged dangerous permission combinations and cut security vulnerabilities by 40%.
