CoupCats

May 11, 2026

coupcats.fly.dev Open coupcats.fly.dev ↗

CoupCats is a web application for visualizing and predicting political coup risk worldwide, built on a dataset of country-level coup probabilities from R models trained on historical event data. A choropleth map layers that forecast against a heatmap of historical coup events, alongside a leaderboard and per-country panels for drilling into specifics, and a predictive mode lets you move sliders tied to individual risk factors to see how the risk landscape shifts.
